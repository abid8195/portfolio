About

A bit about how I work

I'm a Software Engineering graduate (BEng, Swinburne University of Technology) and a recipient of the Swinburne Excellence Scholarship, which covered 75% of my tuition in recognition of academic merit.

I build full-stack web applications: React and TypeScript on the front end, Node.js/Express, Flask and C# on the back end. Lately I've focused on AI-integrated products, applications that call the Claude and OpenAI APIs as part of the product itself. My most complete project, Calazm, is an AI nutrition tracker where a vision model reads a meal photo while all of the actual calorie and macro math happens deterministically in code. The model describes, the database quantifies.

Alongside my degree I worked as a Junior IT Solutions Engineer at Aakonsult Services / MULINK Technologies (AWS environments, Microsoft 365 and SharePoint, Salesforce case workflows for 50+ users). Since May 2026 I've also been a Student Solution Engineer and App Developer with BetaBuilders, working with a remote team of 40+ developers on FreeAppStore and FreeGameStore. I'm a Microsoft Certified: Azure Fundamentals (AZ-900) holder.

I like picking up domains I don't already know. I taught myself the fundamentals of quantum computing to build a working quantum machine learning pipeline, and I bring the same methodical, documented approach from three years in fast-paced customer service.
